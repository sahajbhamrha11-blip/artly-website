Privacy & cookies

Information we collect

When you send an enquiry we collect the details you provide: your name, company name (optional), phone number, email address, the product or category you are interested in and your message. We use this information only to respond to your enquiry and to provide the products or information you request.

How we store it

Enquiries are stored securely in our website database and may be forwarded to our team by email. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing. To protect against spam we store a one-way, anonymised fingerprint of the network address used to send the form — never the address itself.

Cookies

This website uses only essential storage: a note in your browser that you have seen our cookie notice and, for site administrators only, a secure login cookie. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. If a location map is shown on the contact page, it is provided by Google Maps and subject to Google's own policies.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information you have sent us. Please contact us.

About this product

The Heritage Hearth Stove pairs a clean, matte black body with a richly detailed door frame. An oval viewing window lets you enjoy the fire, while brushed metal knobs control the door and the ventilated ash section below.

It is supplied on a raised hearth tray with a contrasting trim and its own ARTLY badge — a finished look that sits beautifully in a living room, lounge or reception area.

About this product

The Twin Door Oven Stove combines heating and cooking in one upright unit. The lower door opens to the firebox, while the upper door gives access to a separate oven compartment — both with viewing glass framed in embossed scrollwork.
